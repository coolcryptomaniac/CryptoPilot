# CryptoPilot NDA & data-room process

The Commitment-page checkbox is a **request to begin** a confidentiality/data-room process. Checking it does not itself create a binding NDA.

## Proposed workflow

1. Investor submits a non-binding expression of interest and explicitly consents to founder follow-up.
2. Founder verifies the investor/contact independently.
3. The parties decide whether confidentiality is needed for non-public materials.
4. If needed, counsel-approved NDA terms are exchanged and separately signed by authorized representatives.
5. Only after execution should confidential data-room access be granted.
6. Data-room access should be least-privilege, time-limited, logged and revocable.
7. Binding investment documents, suitability/eligibility checks and settlement instructions are handled separately.

## Suggested data-room sections

- corporate/entity documents and cap table;
- founder/team profiles and employment/IP assignment status;
- product architecture and roadmap;
- security model, audits and incident-response plan;
- financial model, revenue, expenses, runway and use of funds;
- customer/usage metrics with privacy-safe aggregation;
- material commercial agreements;
- grant applications/awards and ecosystem integrations;
- regulatory/tax/legal analyses available to the company;
- CPT/token design documents if ever relevant, clearly distinguishing prototype from issued assets;
- risk register and dependencies;
- source-code access only under separately appropriate controls.

## Confidentiality principles

- Do not put passwords, private keys, seed phrases or production secrets in the data room.
- Redact personal data not required for diligence.
- Watermark/highly restrict sensitive exports when appropriate.
- Keep an access log.
- Revoke access when diligence ends.
- Use legal counsel for the actual NDA and investment documentation.

This repository document is an operational process note, not legal advice or a signed confidentiality agreement.
