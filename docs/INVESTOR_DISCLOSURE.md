# CryptoPilot investor interest — risk & full-disclosure notice

**Status:** informational diligence notice for non-binding expressions of interest. It is not an offering memorandum, securities subscription agreement, token-sale contract, investment recommendation or guarantee.

Before any person makes a binding investment, CryptoPilot/the relevant issuing entity should provide counsel-reviewed transaction documents appropriate to the entity, security/token/instrument, investor type and jurisdictions involved.

## What the current Commitment page does

- records only an **indicative, non-binding expression of interest**;
- asks how an interested party might prefer to settle a future transaction;
- requests contact details for diligence/follow-up;
- may store the submitted payload encrypted in the production database;
- may notify `founder@roamwise.co.in` when the submitter explicitly consents;
- does **not** accept or transfer funds;
- does **not** issue CPT or any other security/token;
- does **not** reserve an allocation;
- does **not** make the displayed aggregate EOI amount funded or verified capital.

## Material risks to consider

A future investment in a startup/crypto project could lose **all invested capital**. Risks can include, among others:

- early-stage business failure and dilution;
- crypto-asset price volatility and liquidity loss;
- smart-contract, bridge, wallet, exchange and custody failures;
- hacks, exploits, key compromise and operational error;
- stablecoin depegs or issuer/counterparty failure;
- regulatory, tax, licensing, securities, AML/KYC/KYB and sanctions requirements changing by jurisdiction;
- third-party API, blockchain or infrastructure downtime/deprecation;
- market manipulation, oracle failures and adverse execution;
- token utility/economics failing to create sustainable demand;
- inability to obtain future financing;
- governance/key-person risk;
- cross-border settlement and banking restrictions;
- no assurance of dividends, yield, token appreciation, redemption or liquidity.

## CryptoPilot-specific current boundaries

- CPT remains a testnet utility prototype; there is no public CPT sale in this repository.
- Unrestricted live trading remains disabled by default.
- Small MicroTrade/Pilot limits are operational/UX controls, not legal exemptions.
- Social/Google login is not sufficient authorization to move funds.
- DEX transactions are intended to remain user-signed.
- Grant-readiness scores are preparation guidance, not funding eligibility decisions.

## Before a binding transaction

A serious investor should expect, as applicable:

1. identity/entity verification and beneficial-owner information;
2. investor eligibility/accreditation/sophistication analysis where required;
3. sanctions/AML/KYC/KYB screening where required;
4. legal entity, cap table and ownership information;
5. counsel-reviewed instrument/subscription/token documents;
6. financial statements/budget/use-of-funds;
7. technology/security architecture and audit status;
8. material contracts and third-party dependencies;
9. tax/regulatory analysis for the relevant jurisdictions;
10. explicit settlement instructions independently verified through an authorized channel.

Never send funds merely because a public webpage, email, chat message or social account displays an address. Independently verify all binding documents and settlement instructions.
